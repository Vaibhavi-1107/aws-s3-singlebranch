🔬 Research · Medical AI
HGC-Net: Hybrid CNN-GraphSAGE-CBAM
✦ 94.89% Accuracy · AUC 0.982 · Paper Under Review

3-stage hybrid system for histopathology classification on the MIDOG 2025 dataset (11,939 patches). ResNet-50 extracts patch-level features, a k-NN GraphSAGE GNN models relational context across tissue regions, and CBAM-1D channel attention refines the representation before classification. Addressed 5.8:1 class imbalance via Focal Loss and targeted augmentation.
